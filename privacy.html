Privacy policy

Cover Art for Sonos · effective 14 November 2026

Cover Art does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no account and no server, and it contains no analytics or advertising.

It runs on your Apple TV and talks only to the speakers on your home network: to read what they are playing, and to send the play, pause, skip and grouping commands you give with the remote. It loads each cover from the address the speakers give for it.

The only things it saves, on the Apple TV itself, are which room you last chose, the network addresses of your speakers, and whether you have seen the remote guide. Deleting the app deletes them.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
